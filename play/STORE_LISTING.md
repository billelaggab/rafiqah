## Full Description

**Rafiqah** is a complete Android app built with Flutter, working 100% offline, designed to be the trusted companion for Arab women through every stage — from menstrual cycle tracking, through conception planning and pregnancy, to postpartum and baby care.

### ✨ Features
- 🩸 Cycle Tracker: interactive calendar, daily symptoms, next-period prediction
- 🌡️ Trying to Conceive: fertile window, BBT chart, countdown to best conception days
- 🤰 Pregnancy: weekly fetal growth, kick counter, contraction timer (5-1-1 rule), food directory (+200 items), emergency routing
- 👶 Postpartum: feeding timer, EPDS screening, baby tracker, WHO growth charts
- 🔒 Privacy first: local-only data, AES-256 encrypted backup to Google Drive
- 📴 Works 100% offline

### 🛡️ Compliance
- Mandatory medical disclaimer on first launch
- No ads in emergency/health screens (Zero-Ad Zones)
- No pseudoscience (no gender-selection algorithms)
- Structurally aligned with GDPR/HIPAA
