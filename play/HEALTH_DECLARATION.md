# Google Play Health Declaration — Answers

Use these exact answers when filling the Health Declaration form in Play Console.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| App category | Health & Fitness (not a medical device) |
| Does the app provide medical diagnosis? | No — educational/informational tools only |
| Does the app share health data with third parties? | No — all data stored locally on device |
| Does the app store health data on external servers? | No — local storage only (Drift/SQLite) |
| Does the app provide cloud backup? | Yes — Google Drive appdata folder, encrypted with AES-256 locally before upload |
| Target audience | General (18+) |
| Does the app collect sensitive health data? | Yes (menstrual, pregnancy, breastfeeding, EPDS) — stored locally only |
