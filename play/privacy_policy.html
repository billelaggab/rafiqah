Privacy Policy — Rafiqah App

Last updated: 2026

1. Data Collection

Rafiqah stores all health data (menstrual cycles, pregnancy, breastfeeding, EPDS) locally on your device only. We do not send any health data to external servers.

2. Advertising (AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the Advertising ID and general device data to serve ads. You can disable ad personalization in Android settings. No health data is shared with AdMob.

3. Backup (Google Drive)

Backup is optional. When enabled, the database file is encrypted with AES-256 locally before being uploaded to the app's hidden folder (drive.appdata) on Google Drive. The encryption key is stored in the Keystore (FlutterSecureStorage) and is never uploaded.

4. User Rights

  • Access: all data is visible inside the app.
  • Export: cycle logs can be copied.
  • Full deletion: the "Delete all my data" button in Settings removes everything with one tap.
  • Opt-out: the app works without Google Drive.

5. Third Parties

We do not share your health data with any third party. Geographic routing uses Deep Links (location is not stored).

6. Contact

For inquiries: support@rafiqah.app

7. Medical Disclaimer

Content is for educational and organizational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
